About this masterclass

Move beyond simple prompting and master the art of AI system design. We will dive into the architectural patterns required to build autonomous, production-ready systems using the Claude ecosystem — from basic chat to complex agentic orchestration.

Why join: You will leave with a clear understanding of the technical skills, protocols and design philosophies required to build reliable, scalable agentic systems in production.

Ideal for: AI engineers, senior developers and solution architects looking to specialise in enterprise-grade Claude deployments.
